derivative works of these lists and are distributed under the same terms.

| Ruleset | Source | Authors | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ads` | [EasyList](https://easylist.to/) | The EasyList authors | [GPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0](https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html) |
| `privacy` | [EasyPrivacy](https://easylist.to/) | The EasyList authors | [GPL-3.0-or-later or CC BY-SA 3.0](https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html) |
| `cookies` | [EasyList Cookie List](https://easylist.to/) | The EasyList authors (Fanboy) | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
| `malware` | [URLhaus Malicious Hosts](https://gitlab.com/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter) | curben / malware-filter, data from [abuse.ch URLhaus](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/) | [CC0 1.0](https://gitlab.com/malware-filter/urlhaus-filter#license) |

Problems with specific filters should be reported to PlainView first, so we
can tell whether the list or our converter is responsible.
