    Custom filters

    One filter per line, in Adblock Plus syntax. Examples:

    ads.example.com                 block a domain everywhere
||tracker.example^$third-party  block a domain when loaded by other sites
example.com##.sidebar-promo     hide an element on one site
##.newsletter-popup             hide an element on every site
@@||example.com^$document       allow everything on a site

      About & privacy

      • No data collection. PlainView has no servers, no analytics and no accounts. It never makes network requests of its own. Settings stay in your browser's local storage.
      • Why it needs access to all sites: to hide empty ad slots and cookie banners on the pages you visit. It does not read or record page content.
      • Limitations: no blocker stops every ad. Sites such as YouTube serve ads from the same servers as their videos and change how they deliver them often, so in-video ads may still appear. Filter lists update when PlainView is updated.
      • Hiding cookie banners removes them from view. It does not accept or reject cookies on your behalf.
